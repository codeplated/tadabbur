# 🌙 Ismail (Ishmael) — إِسْمَاعِيل
### اسماعیل علیہ السلام

> **Also known as:** Ishmael, Dhbihullah (the one to be sacrificed)

---

## ✅ Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** Submitted to the command of sacrifice without hesitation; praised as truthful and a prophet (19:54)

---

## 📖 Story — English

Son of Ibrahim and Hajar, left as an infant in the barren valley of Makkah. The Zamzam well appeared for him. When old enough, Ibrahim told him of the dream command to sacrifice him. Ismail's response was immediate and complete submission: 'Do what you are commanded; you will find me, if Allah wills, of the patient' (37:102). He laid himself down. Allah ransomed him with a great sacrifice. Later he helped his father build the Kaaba. He is the ancestor of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. Complete submission to Allah even in the ultimate test
2. Sabr (patience) declared before the test even begins
3. Father and son both submitted — tawakkul is generational
4. The meaning of Islam — submission — embodied perfectly
