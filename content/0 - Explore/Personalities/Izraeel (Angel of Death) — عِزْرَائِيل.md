## 📖 Story — English

Referenced in the Quran as 'the Angel of Death who has been entrusted with you' (32:11). He takes the souls of the dying by Allah's command. Described in 6:61 as angels who take souls. The Quran describes different levels of experience: souls taken in peace (gently drawn out) for the righteous, souls taken harshly for the wrongdoers.
