## Story — English

Sent to a people who worshipped the idol Ba'l. He called them to abandon it and worship Allah. His people rejected him. He was praised by Allah: 'Peace be upon Ilyas' (37:130) and counted among the excellent servants. His story is brief in the Quran but establishes the continuity of prophetic mission against idol worship.
