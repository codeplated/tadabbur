# ✨ Al-Ghafur — ٱلْغَفُور
### The Most Forgiving

## 📖 Meaning — English

Al-Ghafur is the most frequently mentioned of the divine names of forgiveness (91 times). He forgives massively and comprehensively — covering sins so they are hidden from all. He is often paired with Ar-Rahim: forgiveness leads to mercy.

---

## 📖 معنی — اردو

## 💡 In Daily Life

The sheer frequency of Al-Ghafur in the Quran is itself a message: forgiveness is not a rare exception — it is His primary mode of dealing with sinners who return to Him.
