## Meaning — English

Al-Mudhill humiliates and abases whomever He wills — particularly those who sought honor through rebellion or oppressing others. This is always an expression of perfect justice. No one can permanently humiliate whom Al-Mu'izz has honored.

---

## معنی — اردو

المذل ذلیل کرتا ہے جسے چاہے — خاص طور پر متکبرین کو۔ یہ انصاف ہے، ظلم نہیں۔ جسے اللہ عزت دے کوئی اسے ذلیل نہیں کر سکتا۔

---

## In Daily Life

When you fear humiliation for standing on principle, Al-Mudhill's judgment supersedes all human verdicts. The honor or shame that comes from Him is what lasts.
