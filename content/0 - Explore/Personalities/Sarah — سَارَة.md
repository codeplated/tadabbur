## 📖 Story — English

Wife of Ibrahim and mother of Ishaq. When the angels visited with glad tidings that she would bear a son despite being old and barren, she laughed and said 'Woe to me! Shall I bear a child when I am an old woman and this, my husband, is an old man?' The angels confirmed it was the decree of Allah. A brief moment of doubt quickly corrected — she became the mother of Ishaq, grandfather of the Tribes of Israel.
