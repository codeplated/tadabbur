# Imran — عِمْرَان
### عمران

> **Also known as:** Father of Maryam, Surah Ali Imran is named for his family

---

## Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** A righteous man chosen by Allah; his family is among the chosen (3:33)

---

## Story — English

Father of Maryam, chosen by Allah among the families (3:33). His wife Hannah made a vow to dedicate her unborn child to Allah's service — the child turned out to be a girl (Maryam), not the boy she expected. Hannah said: 'The male is not like the female' — acknowledging her surprise. Allah accepted the dedication and made Maryam among the greatest of people. Imran is the grandfather of Isa through Maryam.
