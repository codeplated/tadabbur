## Story — English

The giant warrior of the enemy forces against Bani Israel. The small believing army (those who passed the river test) faced him. Dawud, a young man, picked up five stones from a river. He killed Jalut with one. Allah gave Dawud kingship and wisdom. The story establishes: small in number but firm in faith overcomes large in number but absent in faith. 'How many a small company has overcome a large company by permission of Allah' (2:249).

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Physical might means nothing before faith
2. Small numbers + tawakkul > large numbers without it
3. The stone that killed Jalut launched Dawud's prophethood
