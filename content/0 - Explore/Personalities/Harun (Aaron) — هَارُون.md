# 🌙 Harun (Aaron) — هَارُون
### ہارون علیہ السلام

> **Also known as:** Aaron

---

## ✅ Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** Prophet and brother/assistant of Musa; tried to stop the golden calf worship but was overwhelmed

---

## 📖 Story — English

Brother of Musa, given as a helper/prophet to assist in the mission to Pharaoh. When Musa went to receive the Torah, he left Harun in charge. During the forty days, the Samiri led the Israelites into golden calf worship. Harun tried to stop them but was nearly killed — he prioritized not causing division over maintaining order. When Musa returned furiously, he grabbed Harun by the head and beard. Harun explained he had tried but feared causing disunity. Musa prayed for both himself and Harun. Harun died before reaching the promised land.

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. Even a prophet can be overwhelmed by a deviated crowd
2. The tension between preventing evil and maintaining community unity
3. Supporting a greater mission is itself a form of nobility
4. Family prophetic pairs — Musa and Harun, a model of partnership
