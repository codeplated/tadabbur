## Story — English

When the flood came and Nuh called his son to board the ark, the son refused — saying he would climb a mountain to save himself. He trusted his own strategy over divine guidance. He drowned. Nuh then interceded with Allah for his son, but Allah revealed that the son was not 'of his family' because he was not of faith. This was a painful lesson for Nuh himself about the limits of parental love and the meaning of 'family' in Islam.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Self-reliance against divine guidance leads to destruction
2. Family in Islam is defined by faith, not blood
3. A prophet's love for his child cannot override divine decree
4. Arrogance in crisis — the mountain vs the ark — is fatal
