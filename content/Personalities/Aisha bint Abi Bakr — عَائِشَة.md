## Story — English

Youngest wife of the Prophet ﷺ and daughter of Abu Bakr. The incident of Al-Ifk (the slander): hypocrites spread false accusations about her. Allah revealed ten ayaat (24:11-20) establishing her complete innocence and condemning those who spread the slander. One of the greatest scholars of Islam — narrated thousands of hadith. The Prophet ﷺ said 'Learn half your religion from Humaira.' She is an Umm al-Mu'minin — a mother of all believers.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Divine vindication — Allah Himself cleared her name
2. Slander (qadhf) is a major sin — the Quran warned severely against it
3. Women as primary transmitters of knowledge — Aisha taught the entire ummah
4. Patience in facing false accusation — trusting Allah's eventual clarity
