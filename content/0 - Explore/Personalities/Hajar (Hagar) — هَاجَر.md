## 📖 Story — English

Wife of Ibrahim and mother of Ismail. Left with her infant in the barren, uninhabited valley of Makkah by Ibrahim on Allah's command. When Ibrahim turned to leave, she asked: 'Did Allah command you to do this?' He said yes. She said: 'Then He will not let us be lost.' She ran between Safa and Marwa seven times in desperate search of water for her dying child. The Zamzam well burst forth. Her run (sa'y) became one of the five pillars of Hajj — preserved for all time. She is the mother of Ismail and grandmother of the Arab prophets.

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. The greatest tawakkul in Quran — 'then He will not let us be lost'
2. Action + trust: she ran (took action) while trusting Allah
3. One woman's act of desperation and faith became eternal worship for billions
4. Asking one clear question — 'is this Allah's command?' — and then fully submitting
