# 🌙 Adam — آدَم
### آدم علیہ السلام

> **Also known as:** The First Human, Abu al-Bashar, Khalifah

---

## ✅ Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** Erred but repented sincerely; chosen, guided, and forgiven by Allah (20:122)

---

## 📖 Story — English

Created from clay as Allah's vicegerent on earth. Taught the names of all things. Commanded to prostrate — Iblis refused. Placed in Jannah with Hawwa, forbidden one tree. Deceived by Iblis, ate from the tree, fell to earth. Repented with the words taught by Allah, was forgiven and chosen as a prophet. His story establishes: the nature of free will, the danger of Iblis, the power of repentance, and the purpose of human existence.

---

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. Repentance wipes out any sin if sincere
2. Iblis's arrogance (refusing to bow to Adam) is the root of all evil
3. Knowledge (of names/reality) is humanity's distinguishing gift
4. The earth is a test, not a punishment
5. Human dignity was established before the first sin
