# Habil (Abel) — هَابِيل
### ہابیل

> **Also known as:** Abel

---

## Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** His sacrifice was accepted; refused to kill in self-defense; the first righteous martyr (5:27-32)

---

## Story — English

Son of Adam whose sacrifice was accepted by Allah while his brother Qabil's was not. When Qabil threatened to kill him, Habil said: 'If you raise your hand against me to kill me — I shall not raise my hand against you to kill you. Indeed I fear Allah, Lord of the worlds. Indeed I want you to take upon yourself my sin and your sin so you will be among the companions of the Fire.' He was killed — the first death in human history. A raven showed Qabil how to bury him. His story establishes the sacredness of human life and the principle that killing one innocent soul is like killing all of humanity.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Refusing to retaliate for a greater principle
2. The first murder in human history — and its eternal condemnation (5:32)
3. Acceptance or rejection of deeds is from Allah — not human approval
4. Killing one innocent is like killing all of humanity (5:32)
