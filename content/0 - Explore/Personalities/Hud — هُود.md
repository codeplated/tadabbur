# Hud — هُود

## Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

## Story — English

Sent to the people of Aad — a powerful civilization in ancient Arabia known for their towering pillars and physical strength. They were arrogant about their power ('Who is mightier than us?'). Hud called them to worship Allah alone, abandon arrogance, and stop oppressing others. They rejected and mocked him. Allah sent a violent wind that destroyed them for seven nights and eight days (69:6-7). Hud and the believers were saved.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Physical strength and civilization are no protection against divine punishment
2. Arrogance about power ('who is mightier than us?') is particularly dangerous
3. The caller's duty is only to convey — results are with Allah
