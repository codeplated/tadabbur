## Arabic

إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦ تَذۡكِرَةٞۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ سَبِيلًا

## English (Sahih International)

Indeed, this is a reminder, so whoever wills may take to his Lord a way
