## Meaning — English

Al-Jami' gathers all things — scattered atoms recreated into bodies on the Day of Resurrection, nations gathered for judgment, contradictory elements harmonized in His plan. 'Our Lord, You will gather mankind for a Day about which there is no doubt' (3:9).

---

## معنی — اردو

الجامع سب کو جمع کرتا ہے — قیامت میں بکھری ہوئی مٹی کو، انسانوں کو حساب کے لیے۔ بکھرے دلوں کو بھی جوڑ سکتا ہے۔

## In Daily Life

When your life feels scattered, Al-Jami' can bring coherence and unity. When relationships are broken, He is the One who can gather hearts back together.
