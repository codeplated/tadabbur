# 👤 Fir'awn (Pharaoh) — فِرْعَوْن
### فرعون

> **Also known as:** Pharaoh, Ramesses II (by some historians)

---

## ❌ Path — Deviated Path | گمراہی

**Reason:** Claimed divinity ('I am your highest lord' 79:24), enslaved Bani Israel, killed male babies, rejected every sign, drowned — his deathbed declaration rejected (10:90-91)

---

## 📖 Story — English

The ultimate symbol of tyranny in the Quran. Claimed to be the highest lord ('Ana rabbukumul ala' 79:24). Enslaved and oppressed Bani Israel. Ordered the killing of all male Israelite babies. Rejected every sign brought by Musa. Called the magicians to defeat Musa — when the magicians believed, he had them crucified. Pursued Musa and Bani Israel to the Red Sea. As he was drowning, he declared: 'I believe that there is no god but the One the Children of Israel believe in.' Allah rejected his deathbed declaration — 'Now? When before you disobeyed.' His body was preserved as a sign for those who come after (10:92). The Quran describes him as 'a sign for those who come after him' — preserved as a lesson.

## 📖 کہانی — اردو

قرآن میں ظلم کی سب سے بڑی علامت۔ 'انا ربکم الاعلیٰ' (79:24)۔ بنی اسرائیل کو غلام بنایا، لڑکوں کو قتل کرواتا رہا۔ موسیٰ کا ہر معجزہ جھٹلایا۔ جادوگروں کو مومن ہونے پر سولی دی۔ ڈوبتے وقت ایمان لایا — اللہ نے کہا: 'اب؟ پہلے کیوں نہ ایمان لائے؟' لاش محفوظ کی گئی نشانی کے طور پر (10:92)۔

---

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. The most powerful human who ever rejected Allah — and his complete end
2. Deathbed repentance is not accepted when it is forced by seeing punishment
3. Allah preserves some bodies as signs — Fir'awn's mummified body is a lesson
4. Power and wealth are not signs of Allah's approval
5. The system of Fir'awn (claiming divine authority over people) is a recurring danger
