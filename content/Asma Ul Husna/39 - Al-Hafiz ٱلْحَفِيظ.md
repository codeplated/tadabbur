# Al-Hafiz — ٱلْحَفِيظ
### The Preserver · The Guardian
#### حفاظت کرنے والا

> **Root:** ح-ف-ظ — To guard, to preserve, to protect
>
> **Category:** Names of Power & Sovereignty · **In the Quran:** 4 times

---

## Meaning — English

Al-Hafiz preserves all things — records of deeds, the heavens and earth, lives until their appointed time, and the Quran itself (15:9). The Quran being preserved perfectly for 1400+ years is Al-Hafiz in action.

---

## معنی — اردو

الحفیظ ہر چیز کی حفاظت کرتا ہے — قرآن کا 1400 سال سے محفوظ ہونا الحفیظ کا عملی ثبوت ہے۔

---

## In Daily Life

Your good deeds are preserved — not one moment of sincerity is lost. And the protection around you daily: the accidents that did not happen, the harm that did not reach you — Al-Hafiz.
