## 📖 Story — English

Created as a companion for Adam. Together they lived in Jannah. Iblis deceived them both into eating from the forbidden tree. Together they repented. The Quran always mentions them as a pair — no blame is placed solely on Hawwa, unlike in some other traditions. She is a symbol of human partnership and joint accountability.
