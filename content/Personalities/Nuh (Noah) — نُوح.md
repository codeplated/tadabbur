## Story — English

Called his people for 950 years (29:14). They rejected him, mocked him, and covered their ears. His own son and wife disbelieved. Allah commanded him to build the ark — he was mocked for it. The flood came and destroyed the disbelievers. His son refused to board, saying he would climb a mountain — Nuh interceded for him, but Allah told him his son was not of his family (by faith). A profound lesson: lineage does not guarantee salvation. Nuh is one of the five Ulul Azm prophets.

---

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Patience in dawah — 950 years of calling with almost no results
2. Family bonds do not override faith — Nuh's son and wife were not saved
3. No one is too far to call — but no one is guaranteed guidance
4. Mocking the truth is a sign of a doomed civilization
5. Salvation is by faith, not by birth or relationship to a prophet
