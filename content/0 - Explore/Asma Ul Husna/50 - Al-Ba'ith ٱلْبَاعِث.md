# ✨ Al-Ba'ith — ٱلْبَاعِث
### The Resurrector · The Raiser
#### اٹھانے والا

> **Root:** ب-ع-ث — To send, to raise, to awaken, to dispatch

---

## 📖 Meaning — English

Al-Ba'ith sends prophets to humanity and will resurrect all souls on the Day of Judgment. The resurrection is as easy for Al-Ba'ith as the first creation. 'Did you think We created you without purpose?' (23:115) — Al-Ba'ith guarantees the return.

---

## 📖 معنی — اردو

## 💡 In Daily Life

Every morning when you wake from sleep is a small reminder that resurrection is real. Sleep is the minor death; waking is the minor resurrection.
