# 👤 Azar (Father of Ibrahim) — آزَر
### آزر

> **Also known as:** Terah (in some traditions)

---

## ❌ Path — Deviated Path | گمراہی

**Reason:** Idol-maker who rejected his own son's sincere call to tawheed; died on shirk (9:114)

---

## 📖 Story — English

The father (or uncle by some interpretations) of Ibrahim who carved and worshipped idols. Ibrahim called him with extraordinary gentleness — 'O dear father' (ya abati) — multiple times. He refused repeatedly and finally threatened to stone Ibrahim and told him to leave. Ibrahim left, promising to seek forgiveness for him. Later, when Ibrahim discovered his father had died on shirk, he disassociated from him as Allah commanded (9:114). A profound lesson in da'wah to family — never harsh, always gentle — but also in accepting that guidance is from Allah alone.

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. Call family with extreme gentleness — Ibrahim's model of 'ya abati'
2. You cannot guide whom you love — guidance is from Allah alone
3. After death on disbelief, loyalty to Allah supersedes family loyalty
4. Gentleness in da'wah is a prophetic method, even when rejected
