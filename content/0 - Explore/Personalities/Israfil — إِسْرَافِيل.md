## ✅ Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** The angel who will blow the trumpet (Sur) to end creation and begin resurrection

---

## 📖 Story — English

Not named in the Quran but extensively referenced as the blower of the Sur (trumpet/horn). The Quran frequently mentions the 'Sur' being blown as the signal of the Day of Judgment and resurrection. He has been holding the trumpet at his lips since creation, waiting for the command. At the first blow — all in the heavens and earth die (except whom Allah wills). At the second blow — all are resurrected.
