# 🌙 Yusuf (Joseph) — يُوسُف
### یوسف علیہ السلام

> **Also known as:** Joseph, Ahsanul Qasas (Best of Stories)

---

## ✅ Path — Straight Path | صراط مستقیم

**Reason:** Maintained faith, chastity, and forgiveness through betrayal, slavery, imprisonment, and power

---

## 📖 Story — English

The most complete human story in the Quran — the entire Surah Yusuf is his story, called 'the best of stories' (12:3). Thrown into a well by jealous brothers. Sold as a slave in Egypt. Bought by a noble household. Seduced by his master's wife (Zulaikha) — he chose prison over sin. In prison, interpreted dreams. Eventually brought before the king to interpret the royal dream. Appointed minister of Egypt's treasury. Reunited with his brothers — who came begging for food — and finally with his father Yaqub. His response to his brothers' betrayal: 'No blame on you today' (12:92). At the pinnacle of power, his prayer was to die as a Muslim and be with the righteous (12:101).

## 📖 کہانی — اردو

کنویں میں پھینکے گئے بھائیوں نے۔ غلام بنا کر مصر بھیجے گئے۔ زلیخا نے بہکانا چاہا — جیل قبول کی گناہ نہ کیا۔ خواب کی تعبیر سے وزیر بنے۔ بھائی فاقہ سے آئے — 'آج تم پر کوئی ملامت نہیں' (12:92)۔ اقتدار کی بلندی پر دعا کی: مسلمان مروں اور صالحین میں شامل کرو (12:101)۔

---

## 💡 Lessons from the Quran

1. Every stage of Yusuf's suffering was a step toward a higher station
2. Choosing prison over sin — the greatest choice
3. Forgiveness at the height of power is the mark of true nobility
4. Never use power for revenge — use it for mercy
5. Dreams are real — divine communication through visions
6. Sabr without despair leads to the most beautiful outcomes
