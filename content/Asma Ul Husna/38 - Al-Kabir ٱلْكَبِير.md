# Al-Kabir — ٱلْكَبِير
### The Great
#### بڑا

> **Root:** ك-ب-ر — Greatness in dignity and magnitude
>
> **Category:** Names of Power & Sovereignty · **In the Quran:** 6 times

---

## Meaning — English

Al-Kabir's greatness is inherent, ancient, and absolute — greatness in dignity, in existence, in authority. It is His natural state of being — not something achieved or that can be diminished. Paired with Al-Ali: 'the Most High, the Great' (13:9; 22:62; 31:30).

---

## معنی — اردو

الکبیر کی عظمت ذاتی اور ازلی ہے — یہ کوئی حاصل کردہ یا گھٹنے والی چیز نہیں۔

---

## In Daily Life

Every time something seems overpowering — a problem, an enemy, a system — Al-Kabir reminds you the truly Great One is on your side.
