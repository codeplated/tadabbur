## 📖 Meaning — English

As-Salam is the source and embodiment of all peace — He Himself is free from every deficiency, and He is the giver of peace to His creation. The greeting of the people of Jannah will be 'Salam' (14:23), and Jannah itself is called Dar as-Salam — the Abode of Peace.

## 💡 In Daily Life

Anxiety often comes from seeking peace in things that cannot give it. As-Salam is a reminder that peace is a Person to turn to, not a circumstance to achieve.
