## Story — English

Son of Ibrahim and Sarah, born miraculously to an elderly barren woman. Promised by angels as a bearer of knowledge (37:113). Father of Yaqub (Jacob), grandfather of Yusuf. Ancestor of all the Israelite prophets. His miraculous birth from an old mother confirmed the unlimited power of Allah.

ابراہیم اور سارہ کے بیٹے، معجزاتی ولادت۔ یعقوب کے باپ، یوسف کے دادا۔ بنی اسرائیل کے انبیاء کے جد امجد۔

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Miracles of birth — Allah's power over natural limits
2. Prophecy can run in families — but it is Allah's gift, not inheritance
