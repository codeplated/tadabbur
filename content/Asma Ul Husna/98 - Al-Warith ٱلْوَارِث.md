## Meaning — English

Al-Warith is the ultimate inheritor — He remains after all of creation passes away and everything returns to Him. All that humans accumulate will be 'inherited' by Al-Warith. 'It is We who give life and cause death, and We are the Inheritors' (15:23).

---

## معنی — اردو

الوارث آخر میں سب کا وارث ہے — سب کی جمع پونجی، سلطنتیں، شہرت — سب واپس جائے گی۔

---

## In Daily Life

You are a temporary custodian of your wealth, time, and talents — Al-Warith is the ultimate owner. Spend as a trustee who knows the real owner will reclaim everything.
