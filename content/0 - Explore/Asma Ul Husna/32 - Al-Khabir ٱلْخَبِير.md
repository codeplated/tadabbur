# ✨ Al-Khabir — ٱلْخَبِير
### The All-Aware
#### باخبر

> **Root:** خ-ب-ر — Deep awareness, inner knowledge, expertise
>
> **Category:** Names of Knowledge · **In the Quran:** 45 times

---

## 📖 Meaning — English

Al-Khabir has expert, penetrating knowledge of all things — not just surface awareness but insight into hidden realities. He knows the inner state of hearts, the hidden motivations behind actions. Al-Alim knows everything; Al-Khabir knows everything deeply, inside out.

---

## 📖 معنی — اردو

الخبیر ظاہری علم نہیں بلکہ گہرا باطنی علم رکھتا ہے — دلوں کی کیفیت، اعمال کے چھپے ارادے۔

---

## 💡 In Daily Life

You cannot hide your true intentions from Al-Khabir. But also: He knows the true purity of your intentions even when others judge you by appearances.
