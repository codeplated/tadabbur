## Meaning — English

Al-Muqsit actively implements and enforces justice. The scale of the Day of Judgment (the Mizan) is the instrument of Al-Muqsit — every deed weighed with perfect precision. Not even the weight of a mustard seed of injustice passes in His court.

---

## معنی — اردو

المقسط کامل انصاف نافذ کرتا ہے۔ قیامت کا میزان اس کا آلہ ہے — رائی کے دانے کے برابر بھی ظلم نہیں گزرتا۔

---

## In Daily Life

Perfect justice is rare in this world — but Al-Muqsit's scales are perfectly calibrated. No deed, no suffering, no injustice goes unaccounted for in His court.
