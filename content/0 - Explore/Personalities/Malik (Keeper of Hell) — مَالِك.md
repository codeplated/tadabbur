## 📖 Story — English

Named in the Quran in 43:77 where the people of Hell will call out to him: 'O Malik, let your Lord put an end to us!' He will reply: 'Indeed, you will remain.' He is the stern, powerful guardian of Jahannam who carries out divine justice.
