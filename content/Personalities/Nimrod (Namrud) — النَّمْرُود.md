## Story — English

The king of the time of Ibrahim who claimed to be a god. The Quran records his debate with Ibrahim (2:258): Ibrahim said his Lord gives life and causes death; Nimrod said 'I give life and death' (by releasing and executing prisoners). Ibrahim then said 'Allah brings the sun from the East — bring it from the West.' Nimrod was confounded. He ordered Ibrahim burned — the fire became cool. According to tradition, he eventually died killed by a mosquito that entered his nostril — the mightiest king, destroyed by the most insignificant creature.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Power and wealth breed delusion of divinity
2. Ibrahim's logical argument silenced the most powerful king
3. Allah uses the smallest means to destroy the greatest tyrants
4. Claiming divinity is the ultimate arrogance — and always falls
