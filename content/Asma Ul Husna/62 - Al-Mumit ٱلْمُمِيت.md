# Al-Mumit — ٱلْمُمِيت
### The Taker of Life · The Giver of Death
#### موت دینے والا

> **Root:** م-و-ت — Death, to cause to die
>
> **Category:** Names of the Hereafter · **In the Quran:** not in this form

---

## Meaning — English

Al-Mumit causes death at the appointed time, without fail. No one dies before their appointed time; no one lives beyond it. This understanding liberates from irrational fears — you will not die before your time, and when your time comes, nothing can prevent it.

---

## معنی — اردو

الممیت موت دیتا ہے — مقررہ وقت پر، یقیناً۔ یہ نام آزادی دیتا ہے: وقت سے پہلے نہیں مریں گے، وقت آنے پر کوئی نہیں بچا سکتا۔

---

## In Daily Life

Knowing Al-Mumit controls death completely liberates you from cowardice. You will not die one moment before your appointed time — so act with courage, stand for truth.
