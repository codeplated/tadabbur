## Path — Deviated Path | گمراہی

**Reason:** Hamstrung the she-camel of Allah despite warning; destroyed by a blast three days after the warning

---

## Story — English

A people who carved homes in the mountains. Given the she-camel as a miracle and test. One man (the most wretched among them — 91:12) hamstrung her, though the Quran assigns collective responsibility to them all. They were warned of three days. On the third day the blast came. The Quran references them frequently as a clear sign for those who pass their area (27:52). Surah Ash-Shams (91) devotes its climax to their story.

## Lessons from the Quran

1. Collective responsibility for the act of one — the most wretched represents the community's choice
2. A specific divine sign deliberately destroyed — the ultimate ingratitude
3. Three days' warning — divine justice gives notice before punishment
